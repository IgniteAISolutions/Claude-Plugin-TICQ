# Capture the method

## Purpose

Anything explained to an agent twice will be explained a third time unless it is
written down. Captured once, it works for every person and every agent on the team,
including the next one the team adds.

## Procedure

**1. Spot the repeat.** Someone says "as I said before" or "I keep telling it", or the
same correction appears twice in the shared space or the retros.

**2. Draft the method,** short:
- **What:** the instruction, in one or two lines.
- **Why:** what goes wrong without it, from the real case.
- **How to apply:** when it fires, and one example.

**3. Put it to the owner** of that job (from the roster) to approve, edit or reject.
Nothing goes live without their yes.

**4. Make it live** where the agent reads it: the agent's shared instructions, or a skill
file for a longer method. Post one line in the shared space saying what was added.

**5. Record it** in the Method notes section of `multiplayer-pilot.md`: the method, the
owner, the date, and approved or rejected.

## Quality criteria

- Each captured method has a what, a why and a how to apply.
- Every live method had its owner's yes.
- Done means the method is where the agent reads it and the team has been told.

## Example

Northgate Supplies (invented).

> Priya twice told the agent that "next week" in supplier emails means the supplier's working week, not Monday.
> **What:** read "next week" in a supplier email as the supplier's next working week and give a date range. **Why:** the Lowe order was promised for Monday when the supplier meant Wednesday to Friday. **How:** any supplier date written as "next week" becomes "dd/mm to dd/mm (supplier said next week)".
> Approved by Tom (owner, supplier chase list), 12/11/2026.

## Edge cases

- **The two explanations contradict each other.** Do not choose; put both to the owner.
- **The method is personal preference** for one person's own work. It belongs in their
  own instructions, not the team's.
- **The owner rejects it.** Record why; do not raise it again unless it comes up a third
  time with a new reason.
