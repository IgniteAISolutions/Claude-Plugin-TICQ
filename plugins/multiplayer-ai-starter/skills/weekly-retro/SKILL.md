# Weekly retro

## Purpose

The pilot learns once a week, on purpose. People and the agent say what worked and what
to change, the agent owns up to its own missteps in writing, and the trust ladder and
rubrics move on evidence rather than mood. Saved where other agents can read it, the note
helps the next agent the team adds start further on.

## Procedure

**1. Gather the week.** From `multiplayer-pilot.md`: briefing scores, rubric passes and
fails, trust moves, methods captured, proposals the agent made and what happened to them.

**2. The agent writes its lessons and missteps first.** What it got wrong, what it
learned, what it would do differently. Specific, with the date of each.

**3. Ask the people,** once, as one batch:
- What worked this week?
- What should change?
- Anything the agent should stop doing?
Five questions at most; fewer is better.

**4. Turn each change into an action** with an owner and a date.

**5. Update** the trust ladder (any task that earned a step, or dropped back) and the
rubrics (any miss that needs a new check), each with its evidence.

**6. Write the note** from `../../templates/retro-note.md` at the top of the Retros section of
`multiplayer-pilot.md`, and post a five-line summary to the shared space.

**7. At the 30-day review,** add one decision: widen the pilot, change it, or stop. The
sponsor decides; record it.

## Quality criteria

- Every change has an owner and a date.
- The agent's lessons and missteps are specific and dated.
- Questions to people went as one batch of five or fewer.
- Done means the note is in the pilot file and the summary is in the shared space.

## Example

Northgate Supplies (invented), week of 10/11/2026.

> **Worked:** the briefing's waiting-on line; Tom says he no longer asks where orders are.
> **Change:** add the supplier chase list's top three to Friday's briefing (Priya, by 14/11).
> **Lessons and missteps (agent):** on 11/11 I quoted the autumn price list after the winter list was posted; I now check the list's date first (added to the rubric, check 3).
> **Trust:** briefing at level 1, eight passes running; two more to the step-up review.

## Edge cases

- **Nobody turns up.** The agent writes its own lessons and missteps anyway and posts the
  batched questions; the retro is not skipped, only shorter.
- **The agent has a long list of questions.** Pick the five that unblock the most; the
  rest wait a week.
- **The same change appears three weeks running.** It is not happening; ask the owner
  what is in the way, or move it.
