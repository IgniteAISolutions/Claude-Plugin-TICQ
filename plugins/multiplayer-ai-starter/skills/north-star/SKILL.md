# North star

## Purpose

An agent that knows what the team is aiming at can bring useful work towards it without
being told. One that does not can only wait for requests. People set the north star,
always; agents work towards it. The course's example: an onboarding goal led an agent to
suggest changes to error messages that measurably improved onboarding the next week.

## Procedure

**1. Ask the people for their draft.** One sentence: what the team is aiming at, and why
it matters to the organisation. Do not write it for them; offer prompts if they stall
("what would make this quarter a success?", "what would a customer notice?").

**2. Test it on three things,** and say what each test found:
- **Ambitious:** would hitting it be a real step, not business as usual?
- **Measurable:** one metric, with a baseline today and a target by a date.
- **Grounded:** does it connect to what the organisation exists to do?

**3. Agree a review date** and who reviews it.

**4. Agree who may propose.** Which agents may propose new work towards the north star
without being asked, and who on the team says yes or no to those proposals (from the
roster). "None yet" is a fine answer for a first pilot.

**5. Write it** from `../../templates/north-star.md` into the North star section of
`multiplayer-pilot.md`, and post it where the agent reads.

## Quality criteria

- The words are the people's, and the file says who agreed it and when.
- The metric has a baseline and a target, or is marked directional with the reason.
- A review date and the list of agents allowed to propose are set.
- Done means the agent can read it in the shared space.

## Example

Northgate Supplies (invented).

> **Goal:** every customer knows where their order is without having to ask.
> **Metric:** "where's my order" emails, from about 40 a week now to under 10 by 31/01/2027.
> **Review:** 30/11/2026, the operations director.
> **May propose:** Northgate Ops, to Priya.

## Edge cases

- **Leadership will not commit to a number.** Accept a directional goal for the pilot,
  record the missing number as a gap, and set a date to revisit it.
- **The team asks the agent to write it.** The agent can offer prompts and test drafts; the
  goal itself stays the people's. Say why once.
- **Two goals compete.** Ask which wins when they clash, and write that tie-breaker in.
