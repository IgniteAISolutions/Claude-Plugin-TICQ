# Common first builds, by role

What people in each seat usually turn into a skill first with `/twin`. Use these when
the role map is thin or missing. Offer one, in the person's own terms, and only if
they do that task.

The test for any of them: they have done it three times, it takes more than twenty
minutes, and they could describe a bad one.

**Operations.** The weekly client or project report. The supplier chase list. The
handover note when someone goes on leave. The checklist for a recurring job that has
gone wrong before.

**Sales and account management.** The first draft of a proposal from call notes. The
pipeline update for the Monday meeting. The follow-up after a first meeting. The
"where are we" summary before a renewal.

**Finance and admin.** The month-end pack. The overdue invoice reminder in the firm's
tone. The expense query reply. The board pack summary from the management accounts.

**Leadership.** The weekly priorities note to the team. The one-page brief before a
board or investor meeting. The decision record after a hard call. The reply to a
complaint that has reached the top.

**Marketing and content.** The newsletter draft from the month's notes. The case study
from a project write-up. The LinkedIn post from a meeting insight. The event
follow-up.

**Travel, hospitality and client service.** The itinerary from a confirmed booking.
The pre-departure note to the client. The supplier query in the house style. The
post-trip feedback summary.

**Anyone.** The reply they write ten times a week in different words. The document
they build from the same template every time. The summary someone asks them for
every Friday.
